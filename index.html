Process

How it works

Project-based. You get a locked brief and deliverables before I start— not an open-ended “be my assistant for months” setup.

  1. 1
    Lock the scope

    What “done” looks like, deliverables, what’s in/out, timeline, revision limit.

  2. 2
    Fixed price

    Payment tied to agreed outputs (or a few milestones)—not endless hourly supervision.

  3. 3
    Ship on small jobs

    Files, copy, or working web you can use. Async updates with PH timezone + US/EU overlap.

  4. 4
    Handoff clean

    Organized deliverables and a finish line—book the next short project when you’re ready.

Best fit

  • Fixed-price (or 2–3 milestone) jobs with a brief you can point at
  • Starter / entry-level budgets when the scope is clear
  • Variety: transcript one day, design the next, a small web fix after
  • Clients who want a deliverable—not a dedicated full-time report

Not a fit (by default)

  • Long retainers as the main engagement
  • Vague “help with whatever comes up”
  • Unlimited revisions with no definition of done
  • Guaranteeing results I can’t control (viral growth, rankings, sales)
